## Access requests prepared for approval

### Colombia

First contact the UN Verification Mission in Colombia for a copy or a referral to the archive holding its predecessor mission's records. A parallel Colombian government access route is the Presidency's virtual desk. [UN Verification Mission contact](https://colombia.unmissions.org/en/unvmc-contacto) · [Ventanilla Única Virtual](https://psqr.presidencia.gov.co/Publico/IndexWebPQRS.aspx)

> Solicito copia digital del anexo del informe del equipo de verificaciones citado en la sección 6, páginas 11–12, del Informe de Cierre de Actividades del Mecanismo de Monitoreo y Verificación (septiembre de 2017, documento BAPP 1.03.2868). Es el anexo que describe las 54 violaciones leves y 10 graves al Cese al Fuego y de Hostilidades Bilateral y Definitivo. Si existe un registro tabular asociado, agradecería una copia con los identificadores, la fecha y hora del hecho, la fecha y hora del reporte o verificación si se registraron por separado, el municipio, la parte atribuida y la clasificación. Una versión con datos personales suprimidos sirve para la investigación. Si el anexo se transfirió a otra entidad, agradecería el nombre de la entidad y la signatura o referencia archivística.

### Yemen

Ask the UN Archives and Records Management Section to identify the custodian and advise on access. The Office of the Special Envoy for Yemen is an alternate route for locating the records. [UN Archives contact](https://archives.un.org/en/contactus) · [Special Envoy contact](https://osesgy.unmissions.org/en/contact-us)

> I am requesting the original Hudaydah ceasefire incident registers and associated daily patrol, watch or liaison logs for December 18, 2018 through March 31, 2019. This includes records from the initial UN advance team and Redeployment Coordination Committee before UNMHA's establishment, as well as UNMHA records after its establishment. For each party, please include any available reported and independently observed entries, incident identifiers, occurrence, observation and receipt times, time-zone conventions, attribution and verification status. Please include any reporting-coverage notes that distinguish periods with active monitoring from gaps. A redacted copy or machine-readable export would be suitable for academic research. If the records were transferred during liquidation, please provide the receiving office, accession or series reference, and procedure for requesting access. UN document A/80/7/Add.30, paragraph 81, refers to the planned archive transfer.
