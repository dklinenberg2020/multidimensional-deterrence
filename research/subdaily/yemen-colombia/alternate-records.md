# Additional source review for the pilot windows

## Hudaydah, December 18, 2018 through March 31, 2019

Downloaded Mwatana's historic incident listing around the pilot window and each of the **59** matching public incident pages. The continuous date-sorted listing spans pages 19–22 for the matches, with neighboring pages checked for boundary dates. The 59 IDs are distinct and the detail dates match the listing. Similar incident descriptions may still describe related events. The events cover **37 of 104 dates**. [Mwatana incident listing](https://maps.mwatana.org/en/incident?city=YE18&sort=oldest&page=20) · [Individual case example](https://maps.mwatana.org/en/incident/5f197b9a-fdea-4669-8855-4a6889f9c93d)

The source attributes 40 cases to Ansar Allah, 16 to Joint Forces, two to the Saudi/UAE-led coalition and one to the internationally recognized government. Cases include ground attacks, gunfire, detention, disappearance and recruitment. All 59 pages have a victim table. The extract counts victim rows by the source labels and omits personal details. Seven entries lack a district. [Audit](mwatana-hudaydah-audit.json)

**Eighteen records say AM, 18 say PM and 23 give only the date.** The public detail pages have no occurrence clock. Within the same district and date, the extract contains zero cases with different attributed actors across AM and PM. These are selected human-rights cases and archive gaps do not identify days without activity. The extract is useful for comparison across actors and harms. It supplies no minute-level series or reliable move–countermove pairs.

The [humanitarian access extract](yemen-humanitarian-access-events.csv) records a scheduled convoy that did not depart, a fire reported at the Red Sea Mills and WFP eventually reaching the facility. The original UN letter gives 10:00 as the **planned** departure time on December 29. The follow-up UN report confirms the corridor opening did not proceed. The fire notice gives an upper bound on its date from the release date. The WFP visit establishes access on February 26, with no published occurrence clock in the release. The UN Group of Eminent Experts also documents the impoundment of 30 UN medical-supply trucks bound for Hudaydah on February 15. That incident took place in Ibb, outside the governorate, and is retained as a supply-route event. [S/2018/1173, paragraph 10](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/1658309/files/S_2018_1173-EN.pdf) · [S/2019/11, paragraph 5](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=S%2F2019%2F11&t=pdf) · [UN fire release](https://yemen.un.org/en/22988-statement-humanitarian-coordinator-yemen-fire-red-sea-mills-hodeidah-blow-millions-hungry) · [WFP visit release](https://yemen.un.org/en/11467-wfp-gains-access-vital-wheat-stocks-needed-yemens-hungry-people) · [UN Group of Eminent Experts report, note 1252](https://yemen.un.org/sites/default/files/2019-10/A_HRC_42_CRP_1.PDF)

The Arabic Yemeni Center for Human Rights monthly report is retained for reference. Its Hudaydah truce section combines accounts and aggregate claims, with no timed event register found in that section. The downloaded January 2019 UN Panel of Experts report includes timed Hudaydah case studies for 2018 before the ceasefire. Neither supplied the original joint monitoring register. [Arabic March report](https://ychr.org/reports/490/) · [UN Panel report](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/1664359/files/S_2019_83-EN.pdf)

## Colombia, public MM&V case records

The [case extract](colombia-mmv-public-cases.csv) records nine action rows across seven case groups from original tripartite publications. The Santa Rosa communiqué describes both the FARC-EP movement and the subsequent opening of fire by the security forces. The February release and third monthly report contain six additional case groups, one still pending at publication. The El Capricho account also records an order of actions. None of these nine public rows gives a clock. Event sequence is taken from the account where stated. The Conejo entry preserves the report date, the Los Pinos entry preserves the printed department label pending geographical reconciliation, and dispositions follow the source's terminology. These rows cannot establish what fraction of the 64 classified violations they cover without the annex's case identifiers. [Santa Rosa communiqué](https://www.humanas.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/47.-Mision-monitoreo-ONU.pdf) · [Third monthly report, pages 9–10](https://bapp.com.co/archivos/1.03.2857.pdf) · [February 3 release](https://unmc.unmissions.org/es/press-releases/reporte-del-mmv-sobre-un-proceso-en-verificacion-tres-incumplimientos-y-dos)

Downloaded Defensoría del Pueblo early warning 001-17A.I. It independently describes the January 10 Tailandia case and gives two qualitative time-of-day descriptions for related activity on January 12 and 13. The [additional extract](colombia-defensoria-caq-events.csv) preserves these descriptions and separately records a January 13 detention allegation with explicit occurrence uncertainty. The shared January 10 incident remains a single case in the MM&V extract. [Defensoría report, PDF page 4](https://indepaz.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/IR-N%C2%B0-001-17A.I.-CAQ-Cartagena-del-Chair%C3%A1-y-San-Vicente-de-Caguan.pdf)

## Reproduce the Mwatana extract

Saved HTML and PDFs remain in `../deterrence-subdaily-sources/alternate-records/`, outside the repository. The Mwatana row-level extract stays there while redistribution terms are unresolved. The parser requires `lxml`. It reads saved ascending-date listing pages 18–23 and each linked detail page in the pilot window.

```sh
python scripts/extract_mwatana_hudaydah.py ../deterrence-subdaily-sources/alternate-records ../deterrence-subdaily-sources/alternate-records/mwatana-hudaydah-pilot.csv
```
